DATA

This work contains modified Copernicus DEM GLO-30 data.
Original product: Copernicus_DSM_COG_10_N37_00_E110_00_DEM.tif.
Modifications: crop to an 8 km square, resample to a 236 x 236 grid,
use the grid as horizontally compressed, vertically scaled distant context.
The playable 240 x 160 game-meter terrain, its channels, structures and
vegetation are a separate deterministic teaching setting, not a surveyed
reconstruction. Its seam is blended into the distant context for presentation.
Simulation parameters, capacities, lengths and time are illustrative game units.
Terrain binary MD5: 168be7ac5612ec07b1f82f603c874aab.
